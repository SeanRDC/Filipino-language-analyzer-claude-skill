## Analysis
A countdown of iconic Filipino-film fights. Every scene's emotional peak is a **rhetorical-question barrage** (*May kulang ba sa 'kin? … Pangit ba ako?*; *Sana maisip mo rin…* ×4). That repeated frame is also the best decoding tool, because each repetition fixes the ones around it. Code-switching to English (*I was wrong*, *I deserve an explanation*, *Don't make comments about my personal life*) marks the cold or "classy" line in each fight, and *Four Sisters* calls that out directly (*'Wag mo 'kong ma-English-English!*).

## Skill Fixes Made (self-found)
- English narration with Filipino names and loanwords (*Donald Cruz* = John Lloyd Cruz, *click* = *kilig*, *sa Gutan* = *sagutan*, *ang kata ng past* = *ungkatan*).
- *naingit* can be *naiinggit* (jealous), not only *mainit*: decide by context.
